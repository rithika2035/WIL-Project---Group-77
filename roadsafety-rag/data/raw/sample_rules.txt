Learner drivers must be supervised at all times by a fully licensed driver who has held their licence for at least 12 months. Learner drivers must display L plates on the front and back of the vehicle.

P1 (probationary) drivers, identified by a red P plate, are not permitted to use a mobile phone in any capacity while driving, including hands-free functions, hands-free calling, and loudspeaker operation, for the first three years of solo driving.

P2 (probationary) drivers, identified by a green P plate, may use a mobile phone in hands-free mode only if the phone is secured in a commercially designed holder fixed to the vehicle.

The speed limit in a designated school zone in Victoria is 40 km/h during posted times, typically 8:00am-9:30am and 2:30pm-4:00pm on school days, unless otherwise signed.

All drivers and passengers in a moving vehicle must wear a properly adjusted and fastened seatbelt, unless a valid medical exemption applies.

The general blood alcohol concentration (BAC) limit for fully licensed drivers is 0.05. Learner and probationary (P1 and P2) drivers must maintain a zero BAC at all times.
